Fishing-condition data is derived from the open-source FFX|V Fish Tracker App
by icykoneko and contributors, used under its MIT license:
https://github.com/icykoneko/ff14-fish-tracker-app

The bundled fish names, descriptions, icons, journal categories, and catch
metadata are generated from the same tracker data snapshot.

The native FFXIV window is built with KamiToolKit by MidoriKami,
used under its MIT license:
https://github.com/MidoriKami/KamiToolKit

Ordinary-fish catch requirements, plus the live fishing-hole and caught-status
approach, are derived from GatherBuddy under the Apache License 2.0:
https://github.com/Ottermandias/GatherBuddy

Additional fish metadata used by the all-fish guide is derived from
Teamcraft by Flavien Normand and contributors, used under its MIT license:
https://ffxivteamcraft.com

Uncaught fish names stay hidden unless the native Fishing Log has already
revealed the name to the player.
